# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads define user experience strategy, validate usability, and ensure product solutions meet customer needs. They collaborate with Product Managers on requirements and with Developers on implementation feasibility.

### Responsibilities
- Design user workflows and interfaces to meet acceptance criteria
- Conduct usability testing and gather user feedback
- Participate in design reviews and provide design specifications
- Identify and flag usability risks early in development
- Ensure accessibility and design consistency standards are met

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and product usability
- Reduce post-launch usability issues and support burden
- Enable faster user adoption through intuitive design

### Typical Communication
- Design kickoff and requirements review with Product Manager
- Design review sessions with Developers and stakeholders
- Usability test findings and recommendations
- Accessibility audit results and remediation plans

---

## QA/Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads develop test strategies, define quality standards, and validate that products meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Design automated and manual test scenarios
- Validate quality against Definition of Done
- Identify and triage defects with clear reproduction steps
- Recommend go/no-go decisions for release readiness
- Mentor team on testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and reduce post-release defects
- Enable fast feedback cycles through efficient test automation
- Build team confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy definition during planning
- Defect reports and triage in standups
- Quality metrics and test coverage reports
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Tech Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, guide architecture decisions, and ensure code quality and maintainability. They mentor developers and represent engineering in cross-functional discussions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Conduct code reviews and enforce quality standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor and coach developers on technical best practices
- Participate in technology and tool evaluation
- Document technical decisions and design rationale

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate team learning and capability building

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical risk assessments
- Technology roadmap and platform planning

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and escalate blockers
- Coach team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track sprint metrics and velocity trends
- Support project retrospectives and action item tracking
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery cadence and velocity
- Reduce blockers and cycle time
- Build team resilience and continuous improvement mindset

### Typical Communication
- Ceremony facilitation and meeting notes
- Blocker escalations and status updates
- Sprint metrics and burndown reporting
- Retrospective summaries and action items

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide business context, secure resources, and make high-level priority and trade-off decisions for projects.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Allocate budget and resources for projects
- Participate in project initiation and planning approvals
- Provide regular feedback and direction
- Escalate and resolve business-level risks and dependencies
- Champion project outcomes and communicate results to leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Minimize project delays due to resource or priority conflicts
- Build alignment across organization on strategic priorities

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and initiation meetings
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Budget and resource discussions
- Executive reporting and stakeholder communications

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers design and maintain deployment pipelines, manage infrastructure, and enable reliable, frequent releases to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure, deployment environments, and configuration
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows
- Implement security scanning and compliance checks in pipelines
- Support incident response and rollback procedures
- Monitor production systems and alert on issues

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, safe deployments
- Reduce deployment risk and incident impact
- Improve system reliability and observability

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- CI/CD pipeline status and automation updates
- Infrastructure and deployment documentation
- Incident triage and post-mortem involvement
