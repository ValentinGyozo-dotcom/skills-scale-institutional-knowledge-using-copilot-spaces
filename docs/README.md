# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management process documentation. This folder contains the complete playbook for how OctoAcme runs projects, delivers value, and continuously improves.

## Quick Overview

OctoAcme operates projects through a structured, customer-focused lifecycle that emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. The approach is organized around five key phases: **Initiation** (validate business need and align stakeholders), **Planning** (break work into shippable increments with clear acceptance criteria), **Execution** (deliver through daily standups, sprint-based workflows, and automated quality gates), **Release** (deploy to production with pre-verified readiness and stakeholder communication), and **Close & Retrospective** (capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements).

Three core personas drive execution: **Product Managers** define what should be built and prioritize impact, **Project Managers** coordinate delivery and manage risks and timelines, and **Developers** implement features while collaborating on quality and technical risk mitigation. Communication follows a disciplined cadence—daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and monthly stakeholder updates—with a clear escalation path for risks and blockers. All work is tracked through project boards, validated against acceptance criteria, tested automatically in CI/CD, and measured against success metrics defined in the project charter.

OctoAcme embeds continuous improvement through structured retrospectives, blameless incident learning, and a living Risk Register updated weekly. This commitment to psychological safety, transparency, and iterative refinement enables consistent, repeatable project execution and accelerates onboarding for new team members.

## How to Use These Docs

1. **New to OctoAcme?** Start with [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) to understand our principles and key roles.

2. **Starting a new project?** Follow the [Project Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) guide to validate the idea and create a One-pager, then move to [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md).

3. **In execution?** Use [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) for PR workflows, quality standards, and daily team rhythm.

4. **Preparing a release?** Consult [Release & Deployment](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) for checklists and pre-release verification.

5. **Managing risks or escalating?** Reference [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) for templates and escalation paths.

6. **Running a retrospective?** Use [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) to structure the conversation and track action items.
